# Atlas

> Your personal library of meaningful places.

Atlas is a native iOS application for collecting, organizing and sharing locations that matter.

Unlike traditional map applications, Atlas is not designed for navigation.

Its purpose is to help people remember places.

Restaurants.
Viewpoints.
Bookstores.
Museums.
Parking spots.
Camping locations.
Coffee bars.
Architecture.
Hidden gems.

Anything worth remembering.

---

## Philosophy

Every person builds a mental map of the world.

Atlas becomes the digital version of that map.

Instead of hundreds of forgotten screenshots, browser bookmarks, Apple Maps favourites and handwritten notes, Atlas keeps every meaningful place in one organized library.

---

## Core Principles

- Native iOS experience
- Privacy first
- Offline first
- Fast capture
- Beautiful organization
- User owns their data
- No advertisements
- No social media mechanics
